“And here I thought my impression was good. It can certainly fool the rest of my teammates.” The RED Spy took out his own cigarette, leaned on the railing next to him. “How interesting that you could still tell us apart.”“我还以为我的伪装很不错呢。骗过我其他队友肯定没问题。”红队间谍拿出自己的香烟，靠在他旁边的栏杆上。“真有意思，你居然还能把我们分辨出来。”

This could have been problematic, if he hadn’t already ended things with RED’s Sniper. Too slow, RED Spy, as per usual. He flicked ash from his cigarette. “That speaks volumes regarding the quality of your work. Or the quality of your colleagues.”这本来可能会是个问题，如果他还没有和 RED 的狙击手了断的话。太慢了，RED 间谍，一如既往。他弹了弹香烟上的烟灰。“这可充分说明了你的工作质量。又或者，你同事们的水平。”

“I could not say,” said the BLU Spy.“我说不好，”蓝队间谍说。

There was the sound of an explosion from the RED base, in the direction of the infirmary. It seemed the bad doctor decided to light his pipe. Masonry and wood peppered the roof of the covered bridge and splashed into the creek.RED 基地医务室的方向传来一声爆炸。看来那个坏医生决定点他的烟斗了。砖石和木料纷纷砸在廊桥顶上，又溅落进小溪里。

“How annoying,” said the RED, nonchalantly. “I’m so furious I may just…”“真烦人，” RED 漫不经心地说道，“我气得简直要……”

Another explosion, this time from the BLU base. It seemed to originate from where Demoman kept his grenade-slash-scrumpy-making workshop. It had always been an accident waiting to happen. More masonry and steel fell around them.又是一声爆炸，这次是从 BLU 基地传来的。爆炸似乎源自爆破手那间兼做手榴弹和私酿苹果酒的工坊。那地方一直就是个迟早会出事的隐患。更多砖石与钢铁在他们周围砸落下来。

“How unfortunate,” deadpanned the RED. “And so much bigger than that other one.”“真不幸，” RED 面无表情地说。“而且比那一个大得多。”

“I could not say.”“我说不好。”

“Good evening, Spy.”“晚上好，间谍。”

The furlough was put back on, and the teams temporarily came apart as most went away to their homes to enjoy two weeks of (unpaid) vacation. The lack of incoming money was annoying, but anybody who didn’t have a well-padded Swiss bank account only had themselves to blame. The team reconvened at the BLU company headquarters, since the base in New Mexico was still in pieces. Miss Pauling declared unconvincingly that the destructions at the bases lay with faulty gas lines, and that the bases would not be habitable for several months. The blame was laid on a faulty gas line, but Spy could tell that this was a flimsy cover. This despite Engineer having stayed behind to supervise reconstruction. Spy sat with the rest of his fellow BLUs as Miss Pauling explained their new assignments. None of them were fired and the Gravel Wars would continue, just not in New Mexico. All in the freezing middle of nowhere, somehow even more backwoods than the Badlands.
