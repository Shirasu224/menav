And the worst part is, he knows that Scout is unintentionally doing a sleazy tactic that everyone in the negotiation business learnt in their first class–staying silent so the other will talk and because Spy is no longer the strong man he once was, words began to fell out of his mouth like a lamb’s blood from the sacrifice table. When have he gotten so Meek?

 

Not the entire truth anyway, he was careless but not stupid and Scout would probably not appreciate being put on the spot to hear stories from long long ago. 

 

He told him of what the enemy Spy did to him in the tunnel, how he held him down and almost had his way with him but thankfully Scout step in before any shameful act happened. The elder of the two would lean back against his chair and sucks his breath in and a shudder to play up the act of being traumatized. He opened his eyes and smile at Scout, a small whispered of thanks, nothing more, nothing less just so he can get the boy out of his room. 


Scout had the opposite idea and came closer to him. Spy raised an eyebrow, not understanding what he’s trying to do. The young man rubbed his neck again with his free hand and bit his lips. Spy wanted to continue more before Scout asked if he wanted a hug. It was a weird request and asking to hug someone who just told you they were so close to being rape was about the stupidest thing Spy ever witness but, it also show the boy was still… innocent. Spy looked up to the face that is halfway lit up by the fireplace, he hated to admit but there’s a certain charm to Scout. Maybe he would have a chance with Miss Pauling. 


They ended up hugging. An awkward one at that because Spy was still sitting on his chair and Scout had to bend his knees to comfortably wrapped his arms around his comrade. He put his head on top of Spy’s and let his elbows be manipulated around by Spy. They held on to each other for a minute, just a silent hug with nothing but the fireplace crackle. It would’ve been a funny scenario, a thought that would make Spy laugh once he revisited the memory in the future and yet, he felt a lump in his throat–that uncomfortable feeling stirring and twisting in his stomach. He knows the feeling well and shame began to flared up to his face as Scout’s thumb rubbed his ear through the mask. He straightened his back to make Scout stop but that only pushed his face up to the boy’s throat and he could smell the heavy musk of after battle, all that sweat and gravel. He resisted the urge to shudder and quickly reminded himself this was his own offspring. He is better than that. 


They pulled away and looked anywhere else but each other. Scout cleared his throat while Spy quickly finished his cigarette, he needs Scout to get out of his room now instead the boy was still standing near him but this time Spy noticed the stupid grin he has on his face. 

“What are you smiling for?”

 

Scout quickly dropped the expression. 

 

“Sorry, sorry, it’s just that, I-wow-I’m lost for words.” he brought his bandaged hands up together, forming a triangle and stepping side to side. Spy kept his stares and wait as he knows Scout wants to say more even though Spy desperately needs him to shut up and go away. “I don’t know if anyone ever told you but man, you’re pretty.” 


Who raised this boy?! He was being vulnerable with him about the enemy Spy rape attempt on him not even five minutes ago and he decided to do this? Spy was flabbergasted but he was more disappointed by himself for being flattered at that simple compliment. He crossed his arms, a small head tilt and a slight bent forward. 


“Thank you Scout, but I’m old enough to be your father.” 

 

Scout paled and quickly began to spluttered up useless excuses on how he does not see Spy that way and it’s really weird for Spy to say that considering Scout is a very ladies man who is saving himself for the one and only, Miss Pauling. Spy scoffed at that. Thankfully, that comment finally got Scout to moved away from Spy and eventually, he reached the wooden door and closed it behind him, all while blabbering of course. 

Spy sighed, what a dreadful day and it ends with him hating himself even more than the his old friend who tried to assault him in a haunted cave. He opened his silver cigarette case and looked at his own reflection. Pretty. The wrinkles and slight yellowed teeth can still make someone call him pretty. It has been over twenty six years since someone called him pretty. He hated that his stomach seems to flutter at that compliment. Ridiculous, simply ridiculous from having his old comrade as an enemy and being flustered over a simple word. He hoped Scout will forget whatever happened tonight by tomorrow or at the very least keeps his mouth shut from the rest of the team, he doesn’t need Medic to bug him to take out a piece of his brain so he can live trauma-free. 

They say to not let your mind wander off but it just cant be helped, Spy kept replaying the hug in his mind over and over again even when the fireplace started to dim and the cold air starts to slipped in the room. The feeling of Scout’s skin lingers on his lips and he bit them down until the dead skin is no more and it was bleeding. He shouldn’t, he can’t. It’s mortifying, disgusting, immoral. He felt the phantom of the boy’s waist around his arms and it was awfully familiar and Scout’s youthful feature doesn’t make it any better. His smile, oh mon Dieu, he would love to cradle that face early in the morning and kiss his nose.

Spy took out his revolver from his jacket’s pocket and shot himself in the head.
